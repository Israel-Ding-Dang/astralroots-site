Moon in Cancer

Moon in Cancer means the Moon was in Cancer when you were born. In astrology the Moon stands for your inner world; Cancer is a water sign ruled by the Moon, tender and protective.

You feel everything, and you remember all of it.

Your Moon in Cancer is at full strength in its own sign. You register the emotional temperature of every room, mother the people you love by reflex, and keep a private archive of every kindness and every wound. Home is not a place so much as a feeling you can build anywhere, and you need it the way others need exercise. Your tides are real; honour them. The lesson that ripens with age is receiving: letting others hold you with the tenderness you hand out daily.
